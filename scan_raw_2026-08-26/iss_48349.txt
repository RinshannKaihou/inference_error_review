FineGrainedFP8: modules_to_not_convert never matches on text-only loads of multimodal checkpoints (silent quality corruption)
STATE: open
BODY:
**Summary.** `Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP8` ships `quantization_config.modules_to_not_convert`
with multimodal-wrapper paths (`model.language_model.layers...`). When the checkpoint is
loaded through `AutoModelForCausalLM` the text stack is named `model.layers...`, so none of
the exclusion patterns match. The FineGrainedFP8 quantizer then converts modules the vendor
intended to keep in bf16 (e.g. the PLE projections `ple.key_proj` / `ple.value_proj`,
hyper-connection mixers, linear-attention conv/in_proj):
their bf16 checkpoint weights are silently cast to fp8 **without any scale**, corrupting the
values. Nothing fails at load; quality is just degraded (and in our case a downstream dtype
mismatch happened to surface it).

**Repro.** load FP8 repo with AutoModelForCausalLM, inspect
`model.model.layers[1].ple.key_proj` → converted/`fp8` despite checkpoint tensor being BF16
and the module being listed (with wrapper prefix) in modules_to_not_convert.

**Suggested fix.** Prefix-normalize modules_to_not_convert against the instantiated model
(same logic that already remaps checkpoint keys between wrapper and text-only layouts), or
match on suffix segments. Workaround we used: rewrite the list at load time
(`m.replace("model.language_model.", "model.")`) and pass a fresh FineGrainedFP8Config.

---
Found during day-0 bring-up of `Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP8` on transformers main (5.16.0.dev0); all three verified with local patches, model loads and generates correctly with fixes applied. Cross-report on the model repo: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP8/discussions/2
We also have working CPU-offload bounces for the CUDA-only fp8 paths (grouped experts + FP8Linear) that made the 176B-total checkpoint run on a single 96GB GPU + 128GB RAM - happy to open a PR if there is interest.
