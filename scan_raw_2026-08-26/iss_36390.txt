## Describe the bug

DeepSeek-V4-Flash (block-FP8) on 8x MI300X (gfx942), TP8, produces **correct output at short context and progressively degraded output as prompt length grows**. It never crashes, never NaNs, and emits no error — the text simply decays.

Length sweep, single node, greedy (`temperature=0`), same trailing question with increasing neutral filler prepended:

| prompt length | continuation | verdict |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| ~13 tok | `" blue and the grass is green"` | correct |
| ~103 tok | `". The sky is blue"` | correct |
| ~303 tok | `".. A."` | broken |
| ~603 tok | `"..."` | broken |
| ~1503 tok | `"\n.84."` | broken |

Factual probe (prompt ends `"... Rome is the capital of"`, expected `" Italy"`):

| ~130 | ~230 | ~250 | ~270 | ~290 | ~310 | ~330 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ` Italy` | ` Italy` | ` Italy` | ` Italy` | ` Italy` | ` France` | ` Italy` |

Note this is **not a hard cliff at a page/window boundary** — `" Italy"` appears and disappears across 230–330 tokens. The degradation is smooth and probabilistic with length, which looks like a compounding numerical error rather than a discrete indexing bug.

Needle-in-a-haystack: **0/3 hits at every length tested**, including 3k. Above ~2048 tokens the output is additionally **non-deterministic** across identical greedy requests; below that it is deterministic-but-wrong.
