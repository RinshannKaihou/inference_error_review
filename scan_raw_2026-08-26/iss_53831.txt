[Bug]: DeepSeek-V4-Flash tool_choice=auto silently drops tool calls at deep reasoning_effort (xhigh/max) — distinct from #41132/#51914
STATE: closed
BODY:
### Summary

With `--enable-auto-tool-choice --tool-call-parser deepseek_v4 --reasoning-parser deepseek_v4` on DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731, requests with `tool_choice="auto"` and a **deep** reasoning-effort tier (`xhigh`/`max`) return `finish_reason="stop"` with **empty `content` and no `tool_calls`** at a high rate — the model reasons about which tool to call, then emits nothing further. This reproduces on live traffic, not just synthetic probing, and is fully gated by two independent, cleanly-separable variables: reasoning-effort tier and `tool_choice`.

This is not the DSML-markup-leak bug in #51914/#48931/#49117 (no malformed/partial DSML wrapper appears in `content` in most failures — `content` is `null` or whitespace-only) and not #41132/PR #41199 (that was `response_format=json_object` + thinking leaking into the `reasoning` field; already fixed and present in the version below). This looks like a related-but-distinct interaction: the reasoning→action decoding boundary isn't being handled for the *tool-calling* grammar the way it apparently was for the *structured-output* grammar.

### Environment

- vLLM: `0.26.1rc1.dev132+ga4e3552ec`
- Model: `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731`, served as `deepseek-v4-flash-0731`
- Hardware: 2x B200 (sm_100), `--data-parallel-size 2 --enable-expert-parallel`
- Serve flags (abbreviated to the relevant ones):
  ```
  --enable-auto-tool-choice --reasoning-parser deepseek_v4 --tool-call-parser deepseek_v4
  --kv-cache-dtype fp8 --attention-config '{"use_fp4_indexer_cache": true}'
  --speculative-config '{"method":"dspark","num_speculative_tokens":7,"draft_sample_method":"greedy"}'
  ```
  Speculative decoding (DSpark) is enabled. I have **not** isolated whether disabling it changes the failure rate — that requires a restart I haven't done yet. Flagging as an open variable, not a ruled-out one.
