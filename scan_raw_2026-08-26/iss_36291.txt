BODY:
## Summary

On a GB10 (sm_121, aarch64), `--enable-deterministic-inference` **works
correctly on a dense BF16 model** in the same container image, on the same GPU:
identical concurrent requests collapse from 8 distinct completions to 1.

On a **Qwen3.8-27B NVFP4 (W4A4) checkpoint with hybrid Mamba layers and a DFlash2
drafter**, the same flag is accepted, appears as
`enable_deterministic_inference=True` in the resolved `ServerArgs`, and
**temperature-0 outputs remain non-reproducible**.

The flag therefore appears not to cover one or more of those paths, and it fails
**silently** — the server reports the feature as on. A user reading the resolved
args has no way to know the guarantee does not apply to their model.

## Why this is worth a report even though the flag "works"

The failure mode is not "deterministic mode is broken". It is **a coverage gap
that presents identically to success**. That is worse than an error, because the
natural check — confirm the flag was applied — passes.

We spent a substantial amount of time excluding four other explanations before
testing the flag against a model simple enough to isolate the hardware. Anyone
running a quantized or hybrid model on this hardware will hit the same wall, and
the resolved-args check will reassure them at exactly the wrong moment.
