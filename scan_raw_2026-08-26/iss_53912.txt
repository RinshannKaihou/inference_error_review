### 🐛 Describe the bug

**Summary.** `--enable-prefix-caching` together with MTP speculative decoding still corrupts output on hybrid (GDN/Mamba) Qwen3.5-class models in **v0.28.0**. A small fraction of responses degenerate into repeated punctuation (`!!!!…`) or come back empty. This is the same failure as #43559, which was closed as completed on 2026-08-06 — the merged scheduler-side fix (#51113) does not cover it, and the cache-manager-side fixes (#43650, #48375) are still open.

**Model.** Qwen3.5-architecture dense 27B VLM (`Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration`, hybrid: Gated DeltaNet linear-attention layers + full attention every 4th layer, vision tower), FP8 W8A8 compressed-tensors (`FP8_DYNAMIC`), with an MTP head present in the checkpo
