### 55356 [open] ttnn.lgamma is 474,555 fp32 ULP off at x=0.6 and 9% wrong near x=0.5: Stirling's asymptotic series is evaluated at the input with no argument shift, and the error is an even function of (x-0.5)
## Summary

`ttnn.lgamma` evaluates Stirling's asymptotic series directly at the input, with no argument shift. Stirling's series is accurate only for large arguments, so on `0.2 < x < 2` — where the function is most often actually evaluated — the result carries hundreds of thousands of fp32 ULP of error.

Measured on ttsim v1.10.3 (`libttsim_bh.so`, Blackhole), tt-metal `v0.79.0-dev20260902` debs, FLOAT32 in/out, against `math.lgamma` in double precision:

Dense sweep over `(0.05, 0.99)`: peak **9.04e-2 relative at x = 0.5129**. For `x >= 2` the op is accurate to 0-1 ULP, and below `x ~ 0.15` it is also fine, so the defect is confined to the middle of the domain.

The kernel's own comment above the coefficients reads "Minimal coefficients for 0-3 ULP".
